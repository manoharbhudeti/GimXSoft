No. No credit card, debit card, or advance payment is required. You can register your gym and immediately start using all features for 30 days with zero payment.

Yes. Upload an Excel or CSV file, or add members by hand. Member names, phone numbers, join dates, and plan expiry dates all transfer across in seconds.
